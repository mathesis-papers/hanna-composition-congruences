Proof of Hanna's OEIS A389474 formula (4) and extensions of Salerno–Silverman's (2017) integrality argument

Haobo Ma, Wenlin Zhang

Published paper · Version 2 · CC BY 4.0

PDFSourceCitationRepositoryMathesis recordDOILean source

Abstract

We prove Paul D. Hanna's conjecture in OEIS A389474, formula (4), by extending Salerno and Silverman's (2017) integrality argument to implicit composition equations: the normalized solution of A(x^2+2x^3+2A(x)^2)=3A(x)^2 satisfies A(x)^(3k) congruent to A(x^3)^k modulo 3 for every k>=1. A triangular coefficient recursion with even numerators proves integral existence and rational uniqueness. A unit factor proves A389474 (3), the square congruence modulo 9; the same construction proves A389475 (4.a) and (4.b). Odd-parameter extensions and an alternative proof of the Salerno–Silverman prime-degree special case cover the reversion and iteration identities in linked entries A273095, A273320 and A274396. Every integral series also satisfies F(x)^(p^r k) congruent to F(x^p)^(p^(r-1) k) modulo p^r for primes p, r>=1 and k>=0.
